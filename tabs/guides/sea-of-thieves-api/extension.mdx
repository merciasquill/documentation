## Quick Setup

1. Sign in to Mercia's Quill [here](https://auth.merciasquill.com/login).
2. Sign into Sea of Thieves [here](https://www.seaofthieves.com/login).
3. Open the extension from your browser toolbar and interact with the popup to verify that it is up to date.

---

## Core Extension Functionality

This extension reads your Sea of Thieves RAT token and forwards it to Mercia's
Quill's backend services until expiration. This allows accessing your Sea of Thieves data on-demand as needed to provide services. The [Authentication Guide](/tabs/guides/sea-of-thieves-api/authentication)
explains the authentication values, including the RAT token, in greater detail.

<Note>
  Due to the sensitive nature of your Rat Token, several security measures are
  in place to protect your data: all data is encrypted in transit and at rest,
  all databases are IP-restricted.
</Note>

---

## Managing Your Session

The extension session is managed through Mercia's Quill global authentication. When
you sign in to a Mercia's Quill service, the extension uses your most recently
used account. To sign out, interact with the extension popup or visit
[the sign-out page](https://auth.merciasquill.com/logout).
